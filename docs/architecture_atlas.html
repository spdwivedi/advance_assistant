📐 85% Working Limit Calculus

Decouples strict published quotas from active dispatch limits to eliminate 429 errors:

L_working = floor(L_strict * 0.85)
RPM_working = max(1, floor(RPM_strict * 0.85))
TPM_working = max(1000, floor(TPM_strict * 0.85))
RPD_working = max(1, floor(RPD_strict * 0.85))
Example on Gemini 2.5 Flash Free: 15 RPM → 12 RPM, 250K TPM → 212.5K TPM, 500 RPD → 425 RPD.

⚡ Two-Phase Atomic Lease Protocol

Pre-flight worst-case reservation and staggered pacing delay:

T_est_in = max(1, floor(len(prompt) / 3.5))
T_reserved = T_est_in + T_max_out + T_think
Delta_t_pace = round(60.0 / RPM_working, 2) * |L_in_flight|
Post-flight: commit_lease reconciles with exact provider usage_metadata.

🎮 NVIDIA RTX 4060 8.0 GB GDDR6 Hardware Safety Tiers

🟢 SAFE_GPU VRAM ≤ 5.2 GB 1.5B–7B models. Safe for multi-threaded concurrency.
🟡 HIGH_LOAD 5.2 GB – 8.0 GB 8B, 14B Q4 models. Dedicated single-thread batch only.
🔵 PAGED_HYBRID 8.0 GB – 16.0 GB 32B models. PCIe CPU host RAM paging (~12 tok/s).
🔴 OOM_HAZARDOUS > 16.0 GB 70B+ models. Blocked by governor arbitration.
